Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Email address + passwordYour account. Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes — we cannot read them.
WhatsApp number (optional)Only if you choose to leave it for personal training reminders. Used exclusively for those messages, sent personally by the founder — never automated, never marketing lists. Removed on request or when your account is deleted.
Your spoken answers (audio) and their transcriptsTo run the interview and drills, grade your German, and generate your feedback. Audio is processed for transcription and is not published.
Training progress (scores, error patterns, session history, drill results)To personalize your training: find your weakest skill, prescribe the next step, and prove improvement.
Optional self-reported hiring outcomeTo measure whether the training works. Shown publicly only in anonymous, aggregate, or first-name form — never your email.
Payment confirmation (plan, date)To activate your plan. Card data is handled entirely by the payment provider — it never touches our servers. Mobile-money payments are verified manually from the transfer reference.

Processors we use to run the service

Your data is processed by these infrastructure providers, each only for its stated task: Deepgram (speech-to-text and text-to-speech of interview audio), Groq (the AI interviewer's language model; receives transcripts, not audio), ElevenLabs (optional interview voice synthesis), LanguageTool (grammar checking of transcripts), Render (backend hosting and database), and Vercel (frontend hosting). We do not sell your data and there is no third-party advertising or tracking on the service.

Retention & deletion

Your account data and training history are kept while your account exists so your training can build on itself. Email alhassanmaarouf8@gmail.com from your account address to get a copy of your data or to delete your account and its data.

Your microphone

The microphone is used only while a session or drill is running, only after your browser asks for permission, and recording stops when the session ends or you close the drill.
