What we collect and why

DataWhy

Processors we use to run the service

Your data is processed by these infrastructure providers, each only for its stated task: Deepgram (speech-to-text and text-to-speech), Groq and Cerebras (interviewer language generation from transcripts), Google Gemini / Google Cloud (live audio only when that mode is enabled), ElevenLabs (optional voice mode), LanguageTool (grammar checks), Google Fonts (web typography), browser push providers such as Google, Mozilla, Apple, or Microsoft when you enable notifications, Render (backend and database), and Vercel (frontend). We do not sell your data and there is no third-party advertising or tracking on the service.

Retention & deletion

Your account data and training history are kept while your account exists so your training can build on itself. Coaching-chat history is capped and kept only to preserve training continuity. Operational security logs are minimized and retained only as needed to investigate failures or abuse. Email alhassanmaarouf8@gmail.com from your account address to request access, correction, export, or deletion. Deletion covers the account, profile, coaching history, payment records that are not legally required, and unpublished feedback.

Your microphone

The microphone is used only while a session or drill is running, only after your browser asks for permission, and recording stops when the session ends or you close the drill.
