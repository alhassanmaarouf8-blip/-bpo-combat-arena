Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

See the product judge your German before you pay — free, no card

Every new account gets the free level assessment and the written feedback that follows it: your measured level and your first concrete next step. No card is required and no payment is taken to reach it, so you see exactly how this product assesses you before you decide to buy. The live interviews are part of a paid plan.

Payments are non-refundable

Because the assessment and its feedback are free to try first, purchases are non-refundable. Once a plan is paid, the amount is not returned.

No automatic renewals

German Interview Trainer does not charge cards or renew plans automatically. A new period requires a new manual payment that you choose to make — you are never charged without acting.

Technical failures on our side

If a paid live session fails for confirmed technical reasons on our side (no audio, server error), contact us and we will restore the affected minutes or provide equivalent access. This is service recovery, not a cash refund.

Prices include all payment fees

The price shown on the pricing page is the full amount. Card, wallet and bank-transfer processing fees are ours, never added to your total, and paying by card costs exactly the same as paying by wallet or transfer.

Questions about a charge

If a charge looks wrong, contact us first with your payment reference and we will answer within five working days. Raising it with us directly is faster than a bank dispute, and we can correct our own records immediately.
