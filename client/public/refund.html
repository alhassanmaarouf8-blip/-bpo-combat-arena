Refund Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

14-day guarantee on your first purchase

If you buy a plan for the first time and the service is not what you expected, email us within 14 days of the purchase and we will refund it in full. We may ask what didn't work — that feedback makes the training better — but the refund does not depend on your answer.

Renewals

Subscription renewals can be cancelled any time before the renewal date; after a renewal has been charged, we refund it if you request within 7 days and have not used live-interview minutes in that new period.

Technical failures

If a paid live session fails for technical reasons on our side (no audio, server error), the used minutes are restored or the day's session is not counted. Persistent unresolved technical failure of the core service is always grounds for a full refund of the current period.

How to request

Email alhassanmaarouf8@gmail.com from your account email with the payment reference. Card payments are refunded through the payment provider to the original payment method (typically 5–10 business days); mobile-money payments are refunded to the sending wallet.
