1. What German Interview Trainer is

German Interview Trainer is an AI-powered German interview-training service for customer-service job seekers. It provides simulated job interviews with an AI interviewer, spoken-language drills, speech-clarity and grammar feedback, progress tracking, and private tools that can help you organize opportunities you choose to review. It is a candidate-side training and organization tool: German Interview Trainer is not an employer, recruiter, staffing agency, job board, hiring authority, or official language-certification provider. It does not represent or act for any employer or recruiter and does not guarantee that you will receive a response, pass an interview, obtain an offer, or obtain any job.
