# OMNI-PERFORM full learner journey QA — 2026-07-16

## Test contract

- Production: `https://omni-perform.vercel.app/`
- Candidate: fictional Egyptian B1 learner targeting German customer service.
- Evidence rule: a defect counts only when it is reproducible in production, traceable to a concrete code/data path, or a specific standards/logic failure. Preferences and speculative improvements do not count.
- Voice limitation: microphone permission and playback UX are tested in the real browser; typed answers are used for controlled language content because the automation cannot truthfully impersonate physical speech.
- Vacancy target: official AUTO1 Group SmartRecruiters listing for a German-speaking customer-service agent.
