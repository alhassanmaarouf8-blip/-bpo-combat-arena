# Spoken Diagnosis-to-Transfer Loop Closure

**Date:** 2026-07-16  
**Scope:** software and operational readiness for the first frozen spoken criterion  
**Criterion:** `handle-clear-request` in a German customer-service roleplay

## Outcome

The software loop is closed and executable. The product can now capture server-owned spoken
evidence, derive one bounded diagnosis, prescribe the production `druck-leiter` protocol, require
matched plus novel transfer, export a privacy-minimized owner snapshot, blind the review package,
compare two independent qualified raters, adjudicate only disagreements, and calculate aggregate
gates without exposing participant identity or accepting an operator-authored app verdict.

The educational accuracy claim remains open by design. No owner smoke recording, five consenting
target candidates, or two independent qualified German ratings have been supplied. Therefore no
spoken bottleneck, prescription, transfer, teacher-replacement, employment-probability, or
time-to-hire accuracy percentage is claimed.

## What is proven

- The app cannot manufacture a manual spoken-study verdict.
- Typed, thin, interrupted, duplicate, conflicting, mixed-account, mutated, or malformed evidence
  fails closed.
- A completed drill is not improvement.
- A matched retest alone is not transferable mastery.
- Transfer requires a preserved baseline, a later matched improvement, and a still-later novel
  improvement bound to distinct server task contexts.
- The owner snapshot cannot leak the account email, phone, payments, push subscription, mission
  data, free-form correction text, or a raw transcript through its allowlist.

## What is not proven

- Agreement with expert German raters.
- Correct abstention on real target candidates.
- Prescription appropriateness on real target candidates.
- Novel transfer in real target candidates.
- Hiring probability, speed to employment, or superiority to an elite human teacher.

Those are external human-evidence gates, not unfinished software features. The next valid action is
the owner smoke sequence, followed by the preregistered five-candidate study and two blind qualified
ratings. No new product loop should be opened before that evidence exists.
