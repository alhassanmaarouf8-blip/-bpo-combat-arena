## 8. THE CANDIDATE LADDER (owner mission: pass the exam → excel in training → impeccable agent)

### A. PASS THE EXAM (hiring pipeline, Egypt German accounts)
1. **Application + screening call** (German small talk; shift willingness; salary expectation).
2. **Written assessment**: German grammar test + **E-Mail-Schreiben** (confirmed for Concentrix
   Egypt German roles) + typing speed + computer proficiency. ⇒ candidate needs WRITTEN register:
   Anrede (Sehr geehrte/r), Betreff, förmliche Schlussformel (Mit freundlichen Grüßen), klare
   Absatzstruktur, keine Umgangssprache.
3. **German chat round** — live written conversation (speed + correctness under time pressure).
4. **Ops/HR interview in German AND English** — code-switching mid-conversation is tested.
   Question bank: schwieriger Kunde, Stressresistenz, warum Kundenservice, Schichtbereitschaft,
   Gehaltsvorstellung, + ROLE-PLAY (Beschwerde, Erstattungsforderung, Eskalation).
5. Offers often within 1–2 days (Concentrix data); Vodafone DE pipeline: phone screen → HR + TL
   interview (Glassdoor Egypt, 548 questions on file).

### B. EXCEL IN THE TRAINING PERIOD (the 2-month academy)
Industry-standard 3 phases (sources below):
1. **Communication/soft-skills week(s)** — call frame, tone, empathy phrases, active listening.
2. **Product training (1–4 weeks, complexity-dependent)** — the client's products, systems (CRM,
   ticketing), processes. Daily quizzes; graduation exams typically demand ~85%+; attendance is a
   dismissal criterion.
3. **Nesting (2–4 weeks)** — live calls under close supervision; graduation by consistent KPI
   proficiency. Trainees fail on: attendance, exam scores, freezing on live calls, AHT panic.
⇒ App implication: the learner must arrive ALREADY fluent in the call frame + floor vocabulary
(§4) so training bandwidth goes to product knowledge — that's the differentiator.

### C. IMPECCABLE AGENT (what QA actually measures)
**The German call frame** (every call, in order):
1. Begrüßung + Vorstellung (company script) →
2. **DSGVO-Authentifizierung** — CRITICAL German-market specific: BfDI ruled name+Geburtsdatum
   ALONE is INSUFFICIENT (Art. 32 DSGVO); telecom requires Service-PIN/Kundenkennzahl. The agent
   must explain WHY they verify ("zu Ihrer Sicherheit…") and refuse info without it — politely
   declining an unverified caller is a TESTED skill →
3. Anliegen erfassen + aktives Zuhören (paraphrasieren) → 4. Lösung/nächster Schritt (konkret,
   mit Frist) → 5. Zusammenfassung + "Kann ich sonst noch etwas für Sie tun?" → 6. Verabschiedung.
**QA scorecard weights (industry standard):** Opening ~10% · Discovery/aktives Zuhören ~20% ·
Resolution/FCR ~30% · Communication/Klarheit+Empathie ~20% · Compliance/Datenschutz ~10% ·
Closing ~10% — with **AUTO-FAIL items**: skipped verification, rudeness, wrong binding promise,
data disclosed to unverified caller.
**Hold/transfer etiquette:** ask permission before hold ("Darf ich Sie kurz in die Warteschleife
legen?"), give a time estimate, thank for waiting, warm transfer with a handover summary.
⇒ App implication (no new features): scenario keyPhrases can carry verification beats + hold
etiquette; CS_RUBRIC can mirror the scorecard weights; SAG ES RICHTIG absorbs the frame phrases.
